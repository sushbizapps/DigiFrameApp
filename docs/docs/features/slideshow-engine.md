# Slideshow Engine

## MVP

- Transitions, shuffle/random order, timing control
- Fill-screen vs. letterbox/blur-background display modes
- Play videos and animated GIFs inline
- Basic overlays: clock, date

## v2 and beyond — customization

- **Music/soundtrack sync** — background music, optionally beat-matched transitions, or an ambient music library
- **Ken Burns pan/zoom effect**, additional transition styles (cube, dissolve, custom timing)
- **Per-album settings** — different music/speed/transition per album rather than one global setting
- **Captions/story mode** — a text card or map location shown before a batch of trip photos
- **Collage/multi-photo layouts** — 2–4 photos on screen at once instead of one-at-a-time
- **Aspect-ratio aware mixed layouts** — auto-pair two portrait photos side by side to avoid black bars on landscape TVs (may involve saliency-based smart crop/reframe)

## Ambient / smart display extensions

- Time-of-day themes — dim/night brightness scheduling, matte black background at night
- Motion-based wake — only play when someone's in the room (where hardware allows)
- Multi-zone display — split a big TV into a photo panel + calendar/weather widget panel
- Voice control via Google Assistant ("next photo", "pause slideshow")
- Home screen widget showing a rotating photo without opening the app
